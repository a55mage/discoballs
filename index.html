Features

Everything your library needs

New in 2.0 Redesign

A completely redesigned interface: one frosted frame around your music, light and dark themes, a background tinted by the cover that is playing, and a sidebar that folds to icons when you need room.

Smart library

Tracks, albums, artists or folders, as a list or a grid of covers with zoom. Sort by any field, even by date added.

Library health

Find missing covers, missing tags and duplicates, with a guided duplicate review. Favorites and star ratings too.

Online matches

Metadata and artwork from MusicBrainz and iTunes with a before/after comparison, or match a whole album at once.

Batch editing

Edit many tracks at once, read tags from file names and auto-number tracks.

Rename files

Name files from their tags with your own field order and separator, with a live preview.

Undo anything

Every tag change is recorded: undo a single edit or a whole batch from the history.

Now Playing

Cover, synced lyrics and seven visualizers coloured by the cover, alone or combined, plus a full-window mode.

Media keys

Play, pause and skip from the keyboard or your headphones; the track shows in the system's Now Playing.

Equalizer Studio

Custom presets, Auto EQ by genre and volume normalization from ReplayGain tags.

Playlists and queue

Playlists with drag and drop, a queue with history, shuffle and repeat.

Navidrome

Browse and play your Subsonic / Navidrome server; the top bar shows the connected server.

Command palette

Ctrl/Cmd+K to jump to screens, run commands and find tracks.
