DiscoBalls 2.0 is a complete redesign: a new interface, a new library, a new tag editor and many new features.

## New interface
- One frosted frame around the content, light and dark themes, and a background tinted by the cover that is playing (optional).
- Compact layout with a sidebar that collapses to icons, and a top bar that shows the connected Navidrome server.
- On macOS the window buttons sit in the top bar.
- Command palette (Cmd/Ctrl+K) to jump to screens, run commands and find tracks.
- The app opens on the Library.

## Library
- Browse tracks, albums, artists and folders; list or grid of covers, with zoom.
- Sort by title, artist, album, year, genre, format or date added (file date, or the server's date for Navidrome).
- Health filters for missing covers, missing tags and duplicates, with a guided review of duplicates.
- Favorites, star ratings, multi-selection with batch editing and an inspector for the selected track.

## Tag Editor
- Before/after comparison of online matches (MusicBrainz, iTunes).
- Match a whole album to a MusicBrainz release, with a preview before anything is written.
- Tags from file names, auto-numbering, and a history of every change with undo.
- File list sorted by folder, recently added, title or artist.

## Playback
- Now Playing with cover, lyrics and visualizer in any combination, plus a full-window mode.
- Synced lyrics that glide line by line, with the current line highlighted.
- Seven visualizers (Wave, Radial, Spectrum, Ridges, Bloom, Tunnel, Turntable) coloured by the cover.
- Media keys and system controls on macOS and Windows; the current track shows in the system's Now Playing.
- Volume normalization from ReplayGain tags, Equalizer Studio and Auto EQ by genre.
- Playlists with drag and drop.

## Fixes
- The first connection to a Navidrome server after launch no longer fails.
- Clearer errors when a server cannot be reached.

Settings, playlists, favorites and equalizer presets from 1.x are kept.
